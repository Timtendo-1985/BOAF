IMPORTANT — Add Your Logo
=========================

Save your Birds of a Feather logo image as:

  /public/logo.png

The file must be named exactly "logo.png" and placed in this "public" folder.

Recommended: Export your logo at 400×300px (or larger) as a PNG with
a transparent background so it looks great on all post templates.

Once the file is in place, the logo will appear:
  - In the app header
  - At the top of each Instagram post (Classic Cream, Warm Feather, Nature White)
  - In the footer area of the Deep Slate template
